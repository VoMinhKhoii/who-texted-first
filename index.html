Odds and ends

  • Warm-up: the most repeated exact opener is just "hi" (7 couples).
  • Opener length: about 22 characters for men and 21 for women.
  • Rude openers: 6 from women and 4 from men, even though men sent far more openers. That's 10 messages in total.
  • Speedrunners: 7 of the 43 couples with a wedding date married within 7 months of the first text. One took 10 years.
  • Punctuation: ":))" appears in 24% of all chats.
  • 4am: not one first message was sent during that hour.
  • Busted theory: guys seemed likelier to post this when she chased him (41% vs 28%). With chat proof only, it's 28% vs 28%.

About 60 comparisons were run for this section, so a few of the small-number ones will be flukes. The time-of-day, "xinh", "đẹp", "ơi" and likes gaps are the ones that clearly hold up.

What this can't tell you

  • Success rate. Talking stages that fizzled never get posted, so there is nothing to compare against.
  • Whole trend. Only posts with one of the two known cover wordings count, and TikTok stops serving each sound after about 3,000 posts.
